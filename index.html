What we do

Delta Air Solutions Ltd provides professional HVAC services across Auckland and Waikato, for homes and businesses. We specialise in new builds and homes still under construction, where planning the system early saves money and mess later.

Heat pumps & new builds

Our speciality: heat pumps planned into a new build or a home still under construction, so the pipework and wiring go in before the linings do.

  • New builds and homes under construction
  • First homes — sized and placed room by room
  • Daikin, Panasonic and Mitsubishi Electric
  • Replacements and multi-split systems

Ventilation & HRV

  • HRV and equivalent systems
  • Condensation and mould problems
  • Filter changes and servicing

  • Ductwork design and install

Stop moisture this winter

Why ventilation or HRV matters, even in a house that's already warm.

  • Reduces condensation

    Helps prevent moisture building up on windows and walls.

  • A healthier, drier home

    Removes the stale, humid air that causes mould and musty odours.

  • Free quotes, fast install

Warm homes still need fresh air

Running a heat pump without ventilation traps moisture inside. The house feels warm, but the damp has nowhere to go — which is why windows still stream in the morning.

Warm air on one side. Cool air on the other. Delta is the difference between the air you've got and the air you want.
